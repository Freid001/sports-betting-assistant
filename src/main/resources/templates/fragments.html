Guide to Labels and Colours

Stake

Type an amount or use No bet · £5 · £10 · £20 · £50 in the top banner. With a stake, the odds badges show the total you'd get back (stake + winnings).

Odds

The bookmaker's price (the one picked in Refresh Odds), shown as a fraction (or, with a stake set, what you'd get back). The colour is how likely it is, with the bookmaker's margin removed.

1/670% or more: very likely.
4/650-69%: more likely than not.
8/525-49%: could go either way.
13/210-24%: unlikely.
16/1Under 10%: very unlikely.
Chance

How likely an outcome is, by our estimate (the bookmaker's chance nudged by the stats).

Very Likely75%+. Expected to happen about 3 times in 4, or more.
Likely50-74%. Expected to happen more often than not.
Coin Flip35-49%. A real chance either way.
Outsider20-34%. Happens, but usually doesn't.
Long Shot10-19%. Rarely happens.
MoonshotUnder 10%. Very rarely happens.
Price verdict

Whether the odds pay enough for that chance, over many bets like it.

Great ValuePays clearly more than the risk deserves.
Good ValuePays more than the risk deserves.
Possible ValueLooks like value, but there's little data yet, so treat it with caution.
Fair PriceAbout right for the chance. An optional small bet at most.
Small CostSlightly short, but likely to win (35%+). Your framework allows a few of these, capped.
Poor PriceThe odds are too short for the chance: it doesn't pay enough, even if it usually wins.

A value verdict doesn't mean a bet will win: long shots lose most of the time even at a good price.
